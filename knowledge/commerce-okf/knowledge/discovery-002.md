# How do I determine if a merchant is a good fit for Pathao Commerce?

A merchant is a good fit if they: (1) sell across multiple channels (Facebook + physical store, Daraz + own website), (2) have 50+ SKUs that need centralized inventory, (3) use Pathao Courier already and want to expand to online selling, (4) need instant checkout for social media orders, or (5) want to create an online store without technical skills. Merchants who only sell on one channel with <20 products may not need the full platform yet.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Ask about current sales channels and order volume.
- Check if they use Pathao Courier (pre-populated data = faster onboarding).
- Assess SKU count and whether inventory management is a pain point.
- Determine if they need an online store or just checkout links.
- For single-channel small sellers, recommend starting with Instant Checkout only.
