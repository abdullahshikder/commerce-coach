# Can I set up Meta Pixel tracking?

Yes. Navigate to Add-ons → Pixel Tracking. Enter your Meta Pixel ID. The pixel is automatically installed on your Online Store to track page views, add-to-cart events, and purchases. This enables Facebook/Instagram ad retargeting and conversion tracking.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Go to Add-ons → Pixel Tracking.
- Enter Meta Pixel ID.
- Pixel auto-installed on Online Store.
- Tracks: page views, add-to-cart, purchases.
- Enables ad retargeting and conversion tracking.
