# How do I create a warehouse?

Navigate to Warehouse Management in the sidebar. Click "Add Warehouse". Fill in warehouse name, full address (with map location pin), contact person name, and phone number. Submit for courier approval. Warehouse starts in "Pending" status.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Go to sidebar → Warehouse Management.
- Click "Add Warehouse".
- Fill: name, address, contact person, phone.
- Pin location on map.
- Submit — warehouse enters "Pending" status.

## Steps

1. Navigate to Warehouse Management
2. Click "Add Warehouse"
3. Enter warehouse details
4. Pin location on map
5. Submit for approval
