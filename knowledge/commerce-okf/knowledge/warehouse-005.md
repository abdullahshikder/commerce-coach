# How do I view stock per warehouse?

Navigate to Warehouses → select a warehouse → Stock tab. Shows all products stored in that warehouse with current stock levels. From here you can adjust stock, initiate transfers, and view stock history for the warehouse.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Go to Warehouses → select warehouse → Stock tab.
- List: product name, SKU, current stock, last updated.
- Actions: adjust stock, transfer, view history.
- Stock history shows all changes with timestamps.
