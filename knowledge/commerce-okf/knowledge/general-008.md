# Where can I get help?

The Help Center is accessible from the sidebar. It includes: FAQs organized by topic, video tutorials, step-by-step guides, and a contact form for support tickets. For urgent issues, merchants can reach support via in-app chat or the support hotline.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Click Help Center in sidebar.
- Browse FAQs by topic.
- Watch video tutorials.
- Submit support ticket via contact form.
- In-app chat for urgent issues.
