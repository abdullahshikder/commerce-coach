# How do I connect Facebook Messenger?

Go to Add-ons → Facebook Connect. Click "Connect with Meta" to authenticate. Select your Facebook Business Page. Grant required permissions. Once connected, Facebook messages appear in the Unified Inbox.

Product availability: **live**.
