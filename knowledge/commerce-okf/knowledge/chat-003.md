# What is the Unified Inbox?

The Unified Inbox consolidates all conversations from connected channels (Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, and future channels) into a single view. Merchants can filter by channel, search conversations, and respond without switching between platforms.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- All connected channel conversations in one inbox.
- Filter by: Facebook, WhatsApp, or All.
- Search by customer name, phone, or message content.
- Real-time message sync.
- Unified conversation timeline per customer.
