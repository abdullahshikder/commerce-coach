# Can I add notes to an order?

Yes. Open an order → click "Add Note". Notes are internal and not visible to customers. Use notes for internal coordination, special instructions, or tracking order-related communications. Notes are timestamped and attributed to the team member who added them.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Open order detail → click "Add Note".
- Enter note text.
- Note is internal — not visible to customer.
- Timestamped with team member name.
- All notes shown in order timeline.
