# How do I share a checkout link?

After generating an Instant Checkout link, click the share icon. Choose from WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, copy to clipboard, or SMS. The link includes product details, pricing, and the merchant's branding. Merchants can also create QR codes for physical sharing.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- After generating link, click share icon.
- Options: WhatsApp, Messenger, Copy Link, SMS.
- Link includes product info and merchant branding.
- QR code generation available for print/physical sharing.
