# How do I search and filter products?

On the Products page, use the search bar to find products by name, SKU, or description. Filter by: status (Draft/Active/Archived), category, stock level, channel assignment, and price range. Sort by: name, price, stock, date created.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Search bar: name, SKU, description.
- Filters: status, category, stock, channel, price.
- Sort: name, price, stock, date.
- Results update in real-time.
