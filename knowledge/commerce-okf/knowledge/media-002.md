# Can I crop or resize images in the Media Gallery?

Yes. The Media Gallery provides an image crop action. The Product Memo confirms cropping but does not specify resize presets, rotation, compression, or file-size limits.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Open the image in Media Gallery.
- Choose the crop action.
- Apply the available crop and continue with the supported workflow.
