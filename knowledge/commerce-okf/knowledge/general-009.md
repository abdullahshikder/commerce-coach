# What bulk actions are available?

Bulk actions are available on Products, Orders, and Inventory pages. Select multiple items via checkboxes, then apply: activate/deactivate products, change order status, adjust stock, export to CSV, or delete. Bulk actions streamline management for large catalogs.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Select items via checkboxes.
- Click "Bulk Actions" dropdown.
- Available: activate/deactivate, status change, stock adjust, export, delete.
- Apply to all selected items.
