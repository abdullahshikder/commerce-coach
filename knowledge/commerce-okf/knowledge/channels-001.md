# How do I publish products to sales channels?

Go to Products, select a product, click "Publish". Choose the channel (Pathao Shop, Daraz, Online Store). Map the product category to the channel category. Set channel-specific pricing if needed. Click "Publish" to list the product.

Product availability: **live**.
