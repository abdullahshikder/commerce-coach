# Where do I see all my orders?

Go to Orders in the sidebar. Online Store, Pathao Shop, and Daraz orders start in New Orders. Live Chat / Manual Order, and Instant Checkout orders start in Processing. Use the status tabs, filters, order ID, or customer phone number to find an order.

Product availability: **live**.
