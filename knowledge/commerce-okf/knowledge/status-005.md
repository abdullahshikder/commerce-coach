# Can the merchant change the Online Store domain after publishing?

The subdomain (e.g., merchantname.pathao.com) can be changed once from Online Store Settings before publishing. After publishing, the subdomain is locked. Custom domains (e.g., www.merchantstore.com) are supported but require DNS configuration and SSL setup, which may take 24-48 hours to propagate.

Product availability: **live**.
