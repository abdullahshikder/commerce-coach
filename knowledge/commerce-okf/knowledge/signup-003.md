# Can I sign up with my Google account?

Yes. Google OAuth is supported. Merchants click "Sign in with Google", select their Google account, and are redirected to the profile completion step. The Google email is used as the primary contact email.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Click "Sign in with Google" on the signup page.
- Select or enter Google credentials.
- Grant permission to Pathao Commerce.
- Redirect to business profile completion.
