# Is there a global search?

Yes. The search bar in the top navigation allows searching across products, orders, customers, and invoices. Results are grouped by type with quick links to the relevant detail pages. Search is instant and works across all dashboard sections.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Click search bar in top navigation.
- Type query — results appear instantly.
- Results grouped: Products, Orders, Customers, Invoices.
- Click result to navigate to detail page.
