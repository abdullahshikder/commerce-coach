# How does WhatsApp integration work?

Connect WhatsApp Business via Add-ons → WhatsApp Connect. Uses the WhatsApp Business API. All WhatsApp messages appear in the unified inbox. Supports text, images, and documents. Chat-to-order conversion is available.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Go to Add-ons → WhatsApp Connect.
- Complete WhatsApp Business API verification.
- Link WhatsApp Business phone number.
- Messages appear in unified inbox.
- Reply directly from dashboard.

## Prerequisites

- WhatsApp Business account
- Verified WhatsApp Business API
