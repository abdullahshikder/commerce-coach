# How many images can I add to a product?

Each product can have up to 10 images. The first image is the primary/cover image. Images can be uploaded directly or linked from the Media Gallery. Drag-and-drop reordering is supported on the product form. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Up to 10 images per product.
- First image = primary/cover.
- Upload directly or link from Media Gallery.
- Drag-and-drop to reorder.
- Formats: JPG, PNG, WebP.
