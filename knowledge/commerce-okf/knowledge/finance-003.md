# What does the Finance Dashboard show?

Finance provides a unified view of payment and invoice information from Commerce and connected sales channels, including Pathao Shop and Daraz where applicable. Merchants can open details and download invoices where available.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Open Finance.
- Review Commerce and connected-channel payment information.
- Open invoice details as needed.
- Download an available invoice or report an issue from its detail view.
