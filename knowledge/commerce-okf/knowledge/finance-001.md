# Where can I see my invoices?

Open Finance to view payment and invoice information from Commerce and connected sales channels. Open the relevant invoice to review its details, then download it where that action is available.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Open Finance.
- Find the payment or invoice under Commerce or its connected sales channel.
- Open the invoice details.
- Download the invoice where available.
