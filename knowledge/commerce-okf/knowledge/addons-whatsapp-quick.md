# How do I connect WhatsApp via Quick Connect?

Go to Add-ons → WhatsApp → Quick Connect. Click "Connect with Meta". Select your Business Portfolio and WhatsApp Business App. Scan the QR code. Choose phone numbers to connect. The connection is established automatically.

Product availability: **live**.
