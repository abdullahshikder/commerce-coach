# How do I sign up for Pathao Commerce?

Merchants can sign up directly at commerce.pathao.com using a phone number or email. The flow collects business type (individual/business), business name, and primary product category. After OTP verification the merchant lands inside the dashboard.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Navigate to commerce.pathao.com and click "Get Started".
- Choose signup method: phone (OTP) or email (magic link).
- Complete business profile: business type, name, category.
- Verify identity via OTP.
- Merchant is redirected to the onboarding wizard.

## Steps

1. Open commerce.pathao.com
2. Click "Get Started"
3. Select phone or email
4. Enter OTP
5. Fill business profile
6. Land on dashboard
