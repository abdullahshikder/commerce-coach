# How do I set up Meta Commerce?

Go to Add-ons → Facebook Connect → Meta Commerce. Authenticate with your Facebook Business account. Select your Facebook Business Page and Instagram Business account. Products sync to Facebook/Instagram Shops. Orders from Meta platforms appear in your dashboard.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Go to Add-ons → Facebook Connect → Meta Commerce.
- Authenticate with Facebook Business credentials.
- Select Facebook Business Page.
- Link Instagram Business account (optional).
- Products sync to Facebook/Instagram Shops.

## Prerequisites

- Facebook Business Page
- Instagram Business Account (optional)
