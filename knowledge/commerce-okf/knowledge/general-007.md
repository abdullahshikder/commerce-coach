# Where can I update my business settings?

Navigate to Settings in the sidebar. Settings sections: Profile (business name, contact, logo), Notifications (email preferences), Payment (bank details for payouts), Tax (VAT/TIN info), Security (password, 2FA), and Connected Accounts (channel connections).

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Go to Settings in sidebar.
- Sections: Profile, Notifications, Payment, Tax, Security, Connected Accounts.
- Update business info in Profile.
- Configure email notifications.
- Add bank details for payouts.
- Manage security settings.
