# Can I export my data?

Yes. Export options available on: Products (CSV), Orders (CSV), Inventory (CSV), Finance/Payouts (CSV). Click the export icon on any list page. Exports include all filtered data. Large exports may take a few minutes and are available for download from the notification center.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Click export icon on list page.
- Exports filtered data as CSV.
- Available for: products, orders, inventory, finance.
- Large exports: download from notification center.
