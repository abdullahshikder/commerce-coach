# How do I create an Instant Delivery order?

Open Create Order in Instant Delivery. Enter the delivery type, pickup, recipient, delivery, parcel, and payer/POD details. Review the delivery charge and order details, then submit the parcel order.

Product availability: **live**.

## Steps

1. Open Instant Delivery and select Create Order
2. Choose the delivery type
3. Enter pickup, recipient, and delivery details
4. Add parcel size, weight, or photo where applicable
5. Choose who pays and complete the available POD fields
6. Review the charge and submit the parcel order
