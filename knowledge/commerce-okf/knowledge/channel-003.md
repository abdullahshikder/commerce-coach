# What is the Online Store feature?

Online Store lets merchants create a branded e-commerce website hosted by Pathao. No coding required. Merchants can customize the logo, colors, banners, and domain. Products, checkout, and order management are fully integrated. Published stores are live immediately.

Product availability: **live**.

## How it works

- Go to Online Store → Create Store.
- Customize: logo, colors, banner images, favicon.
- Add products to the store catalog.
- Configure payment methods (COD, bKash, card).
- Set shipping zones and rates.
- Click "Publish" — store is live.
- Optional: connect custom domain.

## Steps

1. Navigate to Online Store
2. Click "Create Store"
3. Customize branding
4. Add products
5. Configure payments and shipping
6. Publish store
