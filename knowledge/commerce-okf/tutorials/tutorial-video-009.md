# How do I connect Daraz and import its products?

Connect the Daraz shop first, then open Products → Import Products, select Daraz and the destination warehouse, fetch and select the products, review category mapping and validation, and import them into Commerce.

## বাংলা

### Daraz কানেক্ট করে প্রোডাক্ট import করব কীভাবে?

প্রথমে সঠিক Daraz seller account connect করুন। তারপর Products → Import Products থেকে source হিসেবে Daraz ও destination warehouse বেছে product fetch করুন। প্রয়োজনীয় product নির্বাচন করে category mapping ও validation যাচাই করুন, তারপর Import চাপুন।

Product availability: **live**.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Daraz seller account being connected
- A destination warehouse in Pathao Commerce

## Steps

1. Open the Daraz connection from the dashboard and authorize the intended seller account.
2. Open Products and click Import Products.
3. Select Daraz as the source and choose the destination warehouse.
4. Fetch the products and select the items to import.
5. Review or complete category mapping and validation.
6. Click Import and verify the imported products in Commerce.
