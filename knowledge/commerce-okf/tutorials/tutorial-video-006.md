# How do I connect a WhatsApp Business account?

From Chats, start Connect WhatsApp and continue only with a WhatsApp Business account and Meta Business Portfolio. Complete Meta authentication, choose or connect the business account and phone number, scan the QR code, follow the WhatsApp instructions, select the timezone, review, confirm, and finish.

## বাংলা

### WhatsApp Business account কানেক্ট করব কীভাবে?

Chats থেকে Connect WhatsApp শুরু করুন। Meta authentication শেষ করে business portfolio ও WhatsApp Business account নির্বাচন করুন বা নতুন app connect করুন। phone number দিন, access review করুন, WhatsApp Business app দিয়ে QR code scan করুন, timezone বেছে review ও confirm করে Finish চাপুন।

Product availability: **live**.

## Prerequisites

- WhatsApp Business account
- Meta Business Portfolio
- Access to the WhatsApp phone being connected

## Steps

1. Open Chats and click Connect WhatsApp.
2. Confirm that a WhatsApp Business account and Meta Business Portfolio are available.
3. Complete Meta authentication and continue.
4. Select the shown portfolio and WhatsApp Business account, or choose Connect a WhatsApp Business App.
5. Enter the phone number and review the requested access.
6. Scan the QR code with the WhatsApp Business app and follow its instructions.
7. Select the timezone, review the connection, and confirm.
8. Click Finish and verify the active connection; Integrations offers the same flow.
