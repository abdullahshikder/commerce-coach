# How do I create and share an Instant Checkout link?

Start Instant Checkout from the header, a product card, or a chat. Add an existing or custom product, confirm quantity, price, weight, and warehouse details, add the item to the cart, generate the link, and share it in social posts, comments, captions, or messages.

## বাংলা

### Instant Checkout link তৈরি ও share করব কীভাবে?

Header, product card বা chat থেকে Instant Checkout খুলুন। existing বা custom product যোগ করে quantity, unit price, weight ও warehouse যাচাই করুন, cart-এ যোগ করে Generate Link চাপুন। তৈরি link social post, comment, caption বা message-এ share করতে পারবেন।

Product availability: **live**.

## Steps

1. Open Instant Checkout from the header, a product card, or a customer chat.
2. Search for an existing product or create a custom product.
3. Set the quantity, unit price, weight, and warehouse as required.
4. Add the item to the cart and review the summary.
5. Click Generate Link.
6. Copy and share the link through the appropriate social post, comment, caption, or message.
