# How do I place and track an Instant Delivery order?

Open Instant Delivery → Create Order, complete pickup, sender, recipient, and address details, then choose an available delivery option. Select who pays, add item or collection information when applicable, review and place the order, and follow its status from Active Orders.

## বাংলা

### Instant Delivery order তৈরি ও track করব কীভাবে?

Instant Delivery → Create Order থেকে pickup, sender, recipient ও address details পূরণ করে উপলভ্য delivery option বাছুন। sender না receiver কে payment করবে তা নির্বাচন করুন; প্রয়োজন হলে item value বা collection information দিন। Review Order দেখে Confirm and Place Order চাপুন, তারপর Active Orders-এর Status History থেকে track করুন।

Product availability: **live**.

## Steps

1. Open Instant Delivery from the dashboard sidebar and click Create Order.
2. Complete the pickup, sender, recipient, and customer details.
3. Choose a warehouse or another supported pickup location.
4. Select one of the delivery options available for the entered addresses.
5. Choose whether the sender or receiver pays.
6. Enter the item value; when collecting an order payment, turn on Selling Something, add the item price, and provide the supported payout number.
7. Click Review Order and verify all details.
8. Click Confirm and Place Order, then open the order in Active Orders to review Status History.
