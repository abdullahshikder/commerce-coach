# How do I set up payouts and review payments and invoices?

Add a payout method from Settings by choosing bank or mobile banking and completing the required information. In Finance, review totals and transaction details by order and channel, and open Invoices to inspect or download received invoices.

## বাংলা

### Payment, payout ও invoice manage করব কীভাবে?

Settings থেকে Add Payout Method খুলে bank বা mobile banking বেছে প্রয়োজনীয় তথ্য দিয়ে save করুন। এরপর Finance থেকে total payment দেখুন, Transactions-এ order ও channel অনুযায়ী details যাচাই করুন এবং Invoices থেকে প্রাপ্ত invoice খুলুন বা download করুন।

Product availability: **live**.

## Steps

1. Open Settings and click Add Payout Method.
2. Choose bank or mobile banking and complete the required information.
3. Save the payout method.
4. Open Finance from the sidebar to review total payment information.
5. Use Transactions to inspect order, channel, delivery, and payment details.
6. Use Invoices to review received payments and download a detailed invoice.
