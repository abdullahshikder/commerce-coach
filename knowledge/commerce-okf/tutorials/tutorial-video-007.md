# What signup methods can I use for Pathao Commerce?

The tutorial shows three choices: manual signup, a Pathao Courier account, or a Google account. Manual signup starts with email, then asks for the business name, mobile number, warehouse, and password before OTP verification. Matching Courier information and warehouses may be imported automatically.

## বাংলা

### পাঠাও কমার্সে কোন কোনভাবে sign up করা যায়?

ভিডিওতে তিনটি উপায় দেখানো হয়েছে: manual signup, Pathao Courier account এবং Google account। Manual signup-এ email দিয়ে শুরু করে business name, mobile number, warehouse ও password দিন, তারপর OTP verify করুন। একই email-এর Courier account পাওয়া গেলে তার তথ্য ও warehouse স্বয়ংক্রিয়ভাবে যোগ হতে পারে।

Product availability: **live**.

## Steps

1. Open the Pathao Commerce site and click Get Started for Free or Sign Up.
2. Choose manual signup, Pathao Courier, or Google.
3. For manual signup, continue with an email address.
4. Enter the business name, mobile number, warehouse, and password.
5. Verify the OTP to complete signup.
6. If a matching Courier account is detected, review the imported account and warehouse information.
