# How do I connect Instagram to Pathao Commerce?

Open Chats and start Connect Instagram. Sign in to the intended Instagram account, review the connection permissions, allow the requested access, and return to Commerce to confirm the account is connected. The same connection can also be managed from Integrations.

## বাংলা

### পাঠাও কমার্সে Instagram কানেক্ট করব কীভাবে?

Chats থেকে Connect Instagram চাপুন। সঠিক Instagram account-এ login করে connection permission review করুন এবং প্রয়োজনীয় access Allow করুন। Commerce-এ ফিরে account connected হয়েছে কি না যাচাই করুন; Integrations থেকেও connection manage করা যায়।

Product availability: **live**.

## Prerequisites

- Login access to the Instagram account being connected

## Steps

1. Open Chats and click Connect Instagram.
2. Sign in to the intended Instagram account.
3. Review the connection and permission toggles.
4. Allow the requested access.
5. Return to Pathao Commerce and verify the connected account in Chats or Integrations.
