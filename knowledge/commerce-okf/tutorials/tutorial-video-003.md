# How do I create, customize, and publish an Online Store?

Create the store with a name, subdomain, and pickup warehouse, then use Manage Website to add business details, branding, layout, theme, social links, and policies. Turn on the publish setting when the store is ready. Tracking and ad-catalogue options can be configured afterward.

## বাংলা

### Online Store তৈরি, সাজানো ও publish করব কীভাবে?

স্টোরের নাম, subdomain ও pickup warehouse দিয়ে Online Store তৈরি করুন। এরপর Manage Website থেকে business details, branding, layout, theme, social links ও policy যোগ করুন। সব ঠিক হলে publish setting চালু করে save করুন; প্রয়োজন হলে পরে tracking ও ad catalogue সেট করুন।

Product availability: **live**.

## Steps

1. Open Online Store and start creating a store.
2. Enter the store name and subdomain, choose a pickup warehouse, and save.
3. Open Manage Website and add the description, contact number, and guest-checkout preference.
4. Upload the logo and banner, then choose the website layout and theme.
5. Add supported social contact details and the return and refund policies.
6. Turn on the publish setting and save, then use Visit Website to check the live store.
7. Add Pixel and tracking information if customer activity measurement is needed.
8. Create an ad catalogue and copy its link to Meta Business Manager if required.
