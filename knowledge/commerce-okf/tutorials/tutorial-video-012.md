# How do I create and select a warehouse?

Open Warehouses and choose Create Warehouse. Enter the warehouse name, contact name, phone numbers, full address, and city, zone, and area, then save. Newly created warehouses are reviewed by the courier team; after approval, review the list and set the appropriate warehouse as default.

## বাংলা

### নতুন warehouse তৈরি ও select করব কীভাবে?

Warehouses থেকে Create Warehouse চাপুন। warehouse name, contact name, phone number, পূর্ণ address এবং city, zone ও area দিয়ে Save করুন। নতুন warehouse courier team approve করবে; approval হওয়ার পর তালিকা থেকে প্রয়োজনীয় warehouse default হিসেবে সেট করতে পারবেন।

Product availability: **live-with-dependency**.

## Steps

1. Open Warehouses from the dashboard sidebar.
2. Click Create Warehouse.
3. Enter the warehouse name and contact name.
4. Add the phone number, optional alternate phone number, and full address.
5. Select the city, zone, and area, then save.
6. Wait for courier-team approval and check the status in the warehouse list.
7. After approval, set the appropriate warehouse as default or edit its settings when needed.
