# How do I connect Facebook to Pathao Commerce?

Open Chats and start Connect Facebook. Sign in to Facebook, review the connection settings, select the correct business Page and Meta Business Portfolio, grant the requested access, and save. You can also start the same connection from Integrations.

## বাংলা

### পাঠাও কমার্সে Facebook কানেক্ট করব কীভাবে?

Chats থেকে Connect Facebook চাপুন। সঠিক Facebook account-এ login করে business Page ও Meta Business Portfolio নির্বাচন করুন, চাওয়া permission review করে connection save করুন। Integrations থেকেও একই flow শুরু করা যায়।

Product availability: **live**.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Facebook account, business Page, and Meta Business Portfolio being connected

## Steps

1. Open Chats and click Connect Facebook.
2. Sign in with the Facebook account that manages the business Page.
3. Review or edit the connection settings.
4. Select the intended business Page and Meta Business Portfolio.
5. Review the requested permissions and save the connection.
6. Confirm that Facebook is active in Chats or Integrations.
