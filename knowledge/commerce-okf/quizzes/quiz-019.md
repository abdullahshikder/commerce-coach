# What happens when a product is set to "Archived" status?

## Options

- Product is permanently deleted from the system
- Product is hidden from all sales channels but can be restored
- Product remains visible but cannot be purchased
- Product is moved to a separate archive folder

## Correct answer

Product is hidden from all sales channels but can be restored

## Explanation

Archived products are hidden everywhere but remain in the system. They can be restored to Active or Draft status at any time.
