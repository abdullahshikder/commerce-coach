# When is POD available for an Instant Delivery order?

## Options

- When the receiver pays
- When the sender pays
- For every order
- POD is not available

## Correct answer

When the receiver pays

## Explanation

The Product Memo ties POD to the Receiver paying flow. Sender-payer orders show Item Value instead.
