# A merchant cannot find an invoice from a connected sales channel. What should you check first?

## Options

- The order source, channel, and whether invoice data is available for that channel
- Whether payouts always run weekly
- Whether the merchant has enabled two-factor authentication
- Whether the product image has a CDN link

## Correct answer

The order source, channel, and whether invoice data is available for that channel

## Explanation

The Product Memo says to check the order source, connected channel, and invoice availability before escalating a missing-invoice issue.
